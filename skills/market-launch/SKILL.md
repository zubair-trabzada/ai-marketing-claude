# Product/Service Launch Playbook Generator

Generate a complete, week-by-week launch playbook with tactical plans, templates, checklists, email sequences, social posts, and metrics tracking.

## When to Use
- User is planning a product, service, feature, or offering launch
- User asks for a launch plan, go-to-market strategy, release strategy, or launch checklist
- User wants to coordinate a multi-channel launch campaign
- Triggered by `/market launch` or `/market launch <product description>`

## How to Execute

### Step 1: Gather Launch Context
Collect these inputs (ask if not provided):

1. **What** are you launching? (product, service, feature, course, event)
2. **Target audience?** (demographics, pain points, existing list size)
3. **Primary goal?** (revenue target, signups, downloads, awareness)
4. **Launch date?** (or desired timeline)
5. **Available channels?** (email list size, social following, ad budget, partnerships)
6. **Price point?** (if applicable)
7. **Existing customers/users?** (for beta, testimonials, case studies)
8. **Budget?** (bootstrapped, moderate, well-funded)

### Step 2: Determine Launch Type

| Launch Type | Best For | Key Channel | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product Hunt | SaaS, dev tools, consumer apps | Product Hunt + Twitter/X | 4-6 weeks |
| Email List | Course, info product, SaaS with list | Email | 6-8 weeks |
| Social Media | Consumer product, personal brand | Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram | 4-6 weeks |
| Paid Ads | E-commerce, established product | Facebook/Google Ads | 2-4 weeks |
| Community | Niche product, developer tools | Reddit, Discord, Slack | 6-8 weeks |
| Partner | B2B, enterprise, marketplace | Partner channels | 8-12 weeks |
| Hybrid | High-stakes, multi-channel | Coordinated multi-channel | 8-12 weeks |

### Step 3: Generate the 8-Week Launch Timeline

#### Weeks 1-2: Foundation
**Objective:** Lock in positioning, build assets, set up infrastructure.

- [ ] Define positioning: "For [TARGET] who [PROBLEM], [PRODUCT] is a [CATEGORY] that [KEY BENEFIT]. Unlike [ALTERNATIVE], we [DIFFERENTIATOR]."
- [ ] Create internal launch one-pager
- [ ] Set up landing page / waitlist
- [ ] Set up analytics and tracking (UTMs, conversion goals, events)
- [ ] Create launch-specific email segment
- [ ] Draft all email sequences (see templates below)
- [ ] Brief design on visual assets
- [ ] Identify 10-20 beta testers
- [ ] List 20+ communities where target audience gathers
- [ ] Set up social content calendar

**Deliverables:** Positioning statement, live landing page, drafted emails, beta tester list

#### Weeks 3-4: Audience Building
**Objective:** Build anticipation, grow waitlist, recruit beta testers.

- [ ] Publish 2-3 problem-awareness blog posts / threads
- [ ] Share behind-the-scenes content (building in public)
- [ ] Engage in target communities (provide value, don't pitch)
- [ ] Send personal beta tester invitations
- [ ] Collect early feedback and testimonials
- [ ] Begin influencer/partner outreach
- [ ] Set up viral waitlist with referral rewards
- [ ] Create teaser content (sneak peeks, countdowns)
- [ ] Record demo video or product walkthrough
- [ ] Draft press release or media pitch (if relevant)

**Content Calendar (Weeks 3-4):**
| Day | Content Type | Channel | Theme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Problem-awareness post | LinkedIn/Twitter | Why this problem matters |
| Tue | Behind-the-scenes | Instagram/Twitter | Show what you're building |
| Wed | Educational content | Blog/LinkedIn | Teach something in your space |
| Thu | Social proof | Twitter/LinkedIn | Beta tester quote or result |
| Fri | Teaser/countdown | All channels | Build anticipation |

**Deliverables:** 4-6 content pieces published, beta testers onboarded, waitlist growing, partner commitments secured

#### Weeks 5-6: Pre-Launch Intensification
**Objective:** Maximize anticipation, finalize assets, prep infrastructure.

- [ ] Send pre-launch email sequence to waitlist
- [ ] Increase social posting to daily
- [ ] Publish beta tester case study
- [ ] Finalize pricing and offer structure
- [ ] Create launch-day content package (all posts, emails, graphics ready)
- [ ] Brief partners/affiliates with swipe copy
- [ ] Set up live chat/support for launch day
- [ ] Test all purchase/signup flows end-to-end
- [ ] Prepare FAQ for support team
- [ ] Create urgency mechanism (early bird pricing, limited spots, bonus expiration)
- [ ] Walk through launch day rehearsal
- [ ] Set up real-time metrics dashboard

**Deliverables:** All assets finalized and scheduled, partners briefed, flows tested, support prepared

#### Week 7: LAUNCH WEEK

**Monday - Soft Launch / VIP Access:**
- Send early access to VIPs, beta testers, top waitlist members
- Post: "We're live for our early supporters"
- Collect first-day feedback and testimonials
- Monitor for bugs/issues
- Goal: First 50-100 users/customers

**Tuesday - Public Announcement:**
- Send main launch email to full list
- Publish launch blog post
- Post launch announcement on all social channels
- Submit to Product Hunt (if applicable -- 12:01 AM PT)
- Activate partner/affiliate promotions
- Begin paid ads (if applicable)
- Goal: Maximum visibility and traffic

**Wednesday - Social Proof Push:**
- Share first customer testimonials and results
- Repost customer reactions
- Send "look what people are saying" email
- Post in communities (genuine value, not spam)
- Respond to every comment, mention, question
- Goal: Build momentum through social proof

**Thursday - Objection Handling:**
- Publish FAQ or "everything you need to know" post
- Send email addressing top 3 objections
- Host live Q&A (Twitter Space, LinkedIn Live, webinar)
- Share comparison content (why this vs alternatives)
- Goal: Convert fence-sitters

**Friday - Urgency and Scarcity:**
- Send "early bird pricing ends soon" email
- Post countdown content
- Share final testimonials and case studies
- Activate scarcity mechanisms
- Goal: Drive final wave of conversions

**Saturday/Sunday - Wrap Up:**
- Send "last chance" email for time-limited offers
- Compile launch week results
- Thank early customers publicly

#### Week 8: Post-Launch
**Objective:** Maintain momentum, collect feedback, iterate.

- [ ] Send post-launch survey to new customers
- [ ] Compile and analyze launch metrics
- [ ] Write launch retrospective (worked / didn't / change)
- [ ] Transition to regular pricing
- [ ] Set up onboarding email sequence
- [ ] Plan content calendar based on learnings
- [ ] Follow up with media/partners with results
- [ ] Identify top customers for case studies
- [ ] Plan v2 features based on feedback
- [ ] Set up ongoing marketing engine (content, ads, email nurture)

### Step 4: Email Sequence Templates

#### Pre-Launch Sequence (Weeks 5-6)

**Email 1: The Teaser (2 weeks before)**
Subject: Something big is coming...
Purpose: Build anticipation
Content: Hint at the product, share the problem it solves, tease launch date.
CTA: "Stay tuned" or "Make sure you're on the list"

**Email 2: The Reveal (1 week before)**
Subject: Here's what we've been building
Purpose: Show the product, build desire
Content: Reveal with screenshots/video. Beta tester results. Announce date and early bird offer.
CTA: "Mark your calendar" or "Get notified on launch day"

**Email 3: The Social Proof (3 days before)**
Subject: "[Beta Tester Name] got [Result] in [Timeframe]"
Purpose: Prove it works
Content: 2-3 beta tester testimonials with specific results.
CTA: "Be ready for [launch day]"

#### Launch Sequence (Week 7)

**Email 4: The Launch (Day 1)**
Subject: It's live -- [Product Name] is here
Content: Announce launch. State offer clearly. Early bird pricing/bonus. Direct link.
CTA: "Get [Product] now"

**Email 5: Social Proof Follow-Up (Day 3)**
Subject: People are already seeing results
Content: First-customer testimonials, reaction screenshots, usage stats.
CTA: "Join [X] others who already [outcome]"

**Email 6: Objection Handler (Day 4)**
Subject: "But what if [common objection]?"
Content: Top 3-5 objections answered. Guarantee/risk reversal. FAQ.
CTA: "Try it risk-free"

**Email 7: Urgency Close (Day 5-7)**
Subject: [X hours] left for [early bird / bonus / discount]
Content: Deadline reminder. Recap value. Final testimonial. Single CTA.
CTA: "Last chance to get [offer]"

### Step 5: Social Media Launch Posts

#### Twitter/X Thread Template:
```
Post 1: After [X months/weeks] of building, I'm thrilled to announce [Product Name] is live.
[Product] helps [target audience] [achieve outcome] without [pain point].
Here's the story of why I built it (and what it can do for you): 🧵 1/

Post 2: The problem: [Describe the problem. Make it relatable.]
Post 3: The solution: [What your product does, simply. Include screenshot/demo GIF.]
Post 4: Early results: [Beta tester results, specific numbers]
Post 5: What's included: [Key features as bullet points]
Post 6: Special launch offer: [Pricing, early bird deal, bonus]
Post 7: Try it now: [Link] [CTA]
```

#### LinkedIn Post Template:
```
I just launched [Product Name], and here's why it matters:

[1-2 sentences about the problem]

After [talking to X customers / spending Y months building / experiencing this myself], I realized [insight].

So I built [Product Name] to [specific outcome].

Early users are already seeing:
- [Result 1]
- [Result 2]
- [Result 3]

If you [target audience descriptor], check it out: [Link]

Special launch pricing for the next [timeframe].

#relevant #hashtags
```

```

Caption:
[Hook - first line that stops the scroll]

The solution: [1-2 sentences]
The results: [specific outcomes from beta users]

Launch special: [offer details]
Link in bio to get started.

[15-20 relevant hashtags]
```

### Step 6: Press and Media Outreach

**Press Release Structure:**
1. Headline: [Company] Launches [Product] to Help [Audience] [Outcome]
2. Subheadline with key stat or differentiator
3. First paragraph: Who, what, when, where, why
4. Founder/CEO quote
5. Product details and key features
6. Market context (why now, trend)
7. Customer quote or early results
8. Availability and pricing
9. Company boilerplate
10. Contact information

**Media Pitch Email Template:**
```
Subject: [Angle] -- [Product Name] launches to [outcome]

Hi [Name],

I'm reaching out because you've covered [related topic] and I thought [Product Name] might interest your readers.

[One sentence: what it does and why it's newsworthy]
[One sentence: early traction or results]
[One sentence: what makes it different]

I'd love to offer you [exclusive story / early access / founder interview / demo].

Best,
[Name]
```

### Step 7: Partner Coordination

**Outreach Timeline:**
- Week 3: Initial outreach
- Week 4: Follow up, share product details and demo
- Week 5: Confirm participation, send swipe copy and affiliate links
- Week 6: Reminder with launch day schedule
- Week 7: Day-of coordination, thank you notes
- Week 8: Share results, pay commissions, plan ongoing partnership

**Provide Partners With:**
- Product access (free account or sample)
- Swipe copy for email, social, blog
- Branded graphics and assets
- Unique affiliate/referral link with tracking
- Commission structure or reciprocal promotion plan
- Launch day schedule with specific asks

### Step 8: Launch Metrics Dashboard

**Awareness:** Website traffic (total + by source), social impressions/reach, press mentions, email open rates

**Engagement:** Time on site, pages/session, social engagement rate, email CTR, demo video completion

**Conversion:** Signup/purchase rate, revenue, average order value, cost per acquisition, email-to-conversion rate

**Retention (Post-Launch):** Day 1/7 retention, feature adoption, support ticket volume, NPS score

### Step 9: Budget Allocation Guide

| Budget Level | Allocation |
|---|---|
| **Bootstrapped ($0-500)** | 100% organic: content, communities, email, personal outreach |
| **Moderate ($500-5K)** | 40% paid ads, 30% influencer/partner, 20% tools, 10% design |
| **Well-Funded ($5K-25K)** | 35% paid ads, 25% influencer/partner, 20% PR/media, 10% events, 10% tools |
| **Enterprise ($25K+)** | 30% paid ads, 20% events/webinars, 20% PR, 15% influencer, 10% content, 5% tools |

### Step 10: Post-Launch Analysis Framework

Generate a retrospective covering:
1. **Goal vs Actual**: Hit targets?
2. **Channel Performance**: Which channels drove most conversions?
3. **Email Performance**: Open, click, conversion rates by email
4. **Top Converting Content**: Which posts/pages/ads drove most action?
5. **Customer Feedback Themes**: What are people saying?
6. **What Worked**: Top 3 results drivers
7. **What Didn't Work**: Top 3 things to change
8. **Unexpected Insights**: Surprises from the data
9. **Next Steps**: Immediate actions from learnings

## Guidelines
- Tie every recommendation to the user's specific product, audience, and resources.
- Include copy-paste templates, not just frameworks.
- Incorporate findings from previous skills (market audit, market landing, market brand) if available.
- Time the playbook to the stated launch date and work backwards.
- Always include a "minimum viable launch" option for resource-constrained users.
